SPRINT 1 - HEADLIGHTS

Milestone: Stage 2 - Lighting
Branch: TowhidRahat
Status: DONE (delivered with Stage 2)

================================================================
GOAL
================================================================
Player presses the headlight key. Both headlights turn on: the
bulbs glow and the road in front of the bus lights up. Press again,
both turn off.

Note: this doc was planned with the H key. The built key is L.

================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT (Claude side)
================================================================
New file: Assets/V1.0/Scripts/BusLights.cs (now in Scripts/Bus/)

- Script goes on BusRoot (same object as BusController).
- Checks if the key was pressed this frame, using the same input
  pattern as BusController (Keyboard.current).
- On press, flips a headlightsOn true/false switch and applies it:
    - Turns headlightLeft and headlightRight (Light components)
      on/off
    - Swaps bulbLeft and bulbRight (Renderers) to the "on" or "off"
      material

6 Inspector fields to fill once the script is on BusRoot:
  Headlight Left          -> the left Spot Light
  Headlight Right         -> the right Spot Light
  Bulb Left               -> the left bulb Renderer
  Bulb Right              -> the right bulb Renderer
  Headlight On Material   -> the glowing material
  Headlight Off Material  -> the dark/plain material

================================================================
WHAT RAHAT DID IN THE EDITOR
================================================================
1. Created two small Sphere objects under the bus model, renamed
   Headlight_Bulb_L and Headlight_Bulb_R, placed at the front.
   (The final bulbs are Cube-shaped.)

2. Created two materials:
   Headlight_Off  -> plain dark gray, Emission off
   Headlight_On   -> Emission ON, white or pale yellow

3. Added a Spot Light as a child of each bulb, aimed forward.

4. Put BusLights.cs on BusRoot and filled the 6 Inspector fields.

5. Pressed Play, confirmed bulbs glow and road lights up.
