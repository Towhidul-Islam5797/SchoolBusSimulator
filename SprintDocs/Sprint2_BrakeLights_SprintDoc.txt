SPRINT 2 - BRAKE / STOP LIGHTS

Milestone: Stage 2 - Lighting
Branch: TowhidRahat
Status: DONE (delivered with Stage 2)

================================================================
GOAL
================================================================
While the bus is braking, the two rear bulbs glow red. When braking
stops, they go dark. No dedicated toggle key. This is automatic and
tied to the brake input, the same way real brake lights work.

================================================================
REAL-WORLD RULE BEING FOLLOWED
================================================================
Real brake lights track whether the brake pedal is pressed, not
speed, direction, or gear.

Our bus uses one key (S) for two jobs depending on gear:
  - In Drive or Neutral, S is the brake (slows the bus down)
  - In Reverse, S is the "gas" for moving backward

Original rule planned in this doc:
  Brake lights ON  -> S is pressed AND gear is D or N
  Lights OFF       -> any other case

FINAL BEHAVIOR (changed during the sprint, as delivered):
  Brake lights solid red -> S pressed in D or N
  Reverse lights BLINK   -> gear is R (as soon as R is selected)
  Brake overrides blink  -> S pressed while in R gives solid red
  Lights OFF             -> any other case

This matches a real vehicle: reverse lights come on the instant
Reverse is selected, and the brake overrides them if both are
active.

================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT (Claude side)

Logic added:
  - Every frame, check S (same Keyboard.current pattern)
  - Read gear from BusController
  - Apply the rule above, blinking with a timer in Reverse

DONE CONDITION
================================================================
- Holding S in Drive or Neutral turns both rear bulbs red.
- Releasing S turns them off.
- Selecting Reverse makes the rear bulbs blink.
- Holding S in Reverse turns them solid red.
All confirmed in Play mode.
